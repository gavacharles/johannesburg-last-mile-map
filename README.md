# Johannesburg last-mile connectivity: provisional prototype

An interactive story map from an open-data research prototype. It explores where dense neighbourhoods have a long walk to known public transport.

**The results are provisional.** Open data has no minibus taxi routes, so these maps cannot show where taxis already run. They have not been checked in the field or reviewed by taxi associations, and they are not a transport plan or a set of proposed routes.

## Data sources
- OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
- WorldPop 2020 constrained population (CC BY 4.0), scaled to the Census 2022 city total
- GHSL R2023A population and built-up surface (European Commission JRC)
- geoBoundaries (CC BY 3.0 IGO)
- Meta Relative Wealth Index, via HDX (used in the area screen)
- Basemaps and imagery: Esri and partners
- Map library: Leaflet (BSD 2-Clause)
